Delete your account

You can delete your Gamora account and all your data whenever you want. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

FROM THE APP

  1. Open Gamora and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm with your password or your Google account.

WITHOUT THE APP

Email soporte@example.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Gamora account”. We will confirm your identity and delete the account within 30 days.

WHAT IS DELETED

  • Your sign-in account and profile (name, email, phone).
  • Your transactions, recurring transactions, savings goals and budgets.
  • Your preferences, the assistant history and the records of your consents.

WHAT IS KEPT

Nothing linked to your account. Technical error reports (Crashlytics) do not include your name, email or financial data, and Google deletes them automatically after 90 days.
