GAMORA PRIVACY NOTICE

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · Version 1.1

This notice applies to everyone who uses Gamora, in Mexico, the United States or anywhere else. This is a translation; if it differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version governs.

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

[Nombre completo o razón social], located at [Domicilio], is responsible for processing your personal data in Gamora, a personal finance app. For any privacy matter, write to [Correo de privacidad].

2. DATA WE PROCESS AND WHERE IT COMES FROM

  • Identity and contact: first and last names, email address and, if you provide it, phone number. You give them to us when you sign up; if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from your Google account.
  • Financial data you enter: income, expenses, categories, recurring transactions, savings goals, budgets and preferences (currency, first day of the period).
  • Conversations with the Gamora AI assistant, which are stored in your account until you delete them.
  • Documents you choose to import (bank statements and pay stubs) and the transactions you confirm from them.
  • App preferences: theme, notifications and the consents you give.
  • Technical data: installation identifiers and device integrity checks to protect the service. If the app crashes, a technical crash report (phone model and OS, app version and where the error happened; never your transactions or amounts). App usage data only if you allow analytics.

Gamora does not ask for or store card numbers, bank accounts or your bank passwords, and it does not connect to your bank accounts. Gamora does not intentionally process sensitive personal data; if a document you import contains it, it is not stored (see section 4).

3. WHY WE USE IT

Necessary purposes:

  • Create and protect your account and sign you in.
  • Store and sync your transactions, goals and budgets.
  • Calculate balances, statistics and savings tips inside the app.
  • Record your recurring transactions and send the alerts you turn on (budget, goals, weekly summary).
  • Detect and fix app errors, and prevent abuse and fraud.

Optional purposes (only with your permission, which you can withdraw in Profile at any time):

  • Usage analytics, to understand which parts of the app are used and improve it.
  • Letting Gamora AI use your financial data to give you personalized recommendations.

We do not use your data for advertising or to build profiles for commercial purposes.

4. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND DOCUMENT IMPORT

What you write to Gamora AI is sent to Google's Gemini model to generate the answer. If you allow the use of your financial data, a summary of your transactions, goals and budgets is also sent. If you don't, the assistant answers without knowing your data. Answers may contain errors and are not professional financial advice.

Importing bank statements and pay stubs: when you choose a PDF or a photo, Gamora first reads it on your phone, using the PDF text or Google ML Kit text recognition, which works offline; in that case the document never leaves your phone. Only if the format is not recognized is the document sent to Gemini to extract the transactions. If the PDF is password-protected, it is opened on your phone and, when it has to be sent, an unprotected copy is sent. Gamora does not store the file, the copy or the password, on any server or on your phone; it only stores the transactions you review and confirm. These documents may contain data such as your account number, address or tax ID (RFC). Payroll XML files (CFDI) are read on your phone and are not sent to Gemini.

5. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH AND TRANSFERS

We use Google services as processors that handle data only on Gamora's behalf: Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (cloud storage), Firebase App Check and Play Integrity (security), Firebase Crashlytics (error reports), Google Analytics for Firebase (only if you allow it) and Gemini through Firebase AI Logic (assistant). Their servers may be located outside your country, for example in the United States. Sharing data with these processors does not require your consent.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not transfer it to third parties for their own purposes. We would only disclose it to an authority when a law or court order requires it.

6. HOW IT IS PROTECTED

Your data is stored linked to your account and encrypted in transit. Database security rules prevent other people from reading or changing your information, and we use device integrity checks to help block requests that do not come from the original app. You can also lock the app with your fingerprint, face or PIN. No measure is foolproof; if a breach affecting your rights occurs, we will notify you without undue delay.

7. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

  • Your account and data: as long as you have an account. They are deleted immediately when you delete it.
  • Gamora AI conversations: until you delete them or your account.
  • Error reports (Crashlytics): 90 days.
  • Analytics data (if you allowed it): the period set in Google Analytics, no longer than 14 months.
  • Records of your consents: as long as your account exists, to show what you agreed to.

8. DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

You can delete your account and all your information at any time from Profile › Delete my data. Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you cannot access the app, write to [Correo de privacidad] from your account's email address.

9. YOUR RIGHTS (ARCO)

You can access, correct and delete your data or object to its processing, and withdraw your consent or limit its use. Many of these changes can be made directly in Profile. For anything else, send your request to [Correo de privacidad] with your name, your account email and what you are asking for; we will reply within 20 business days. If you believe we did not handle your request or that your data was misused, you can contact the personal data protection authority in Mexico (the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno).

10. PEOPLE IN THE UNITED STATES

  • Categories of data: identifiers (name, email, optional phone number, account and installation IDs), financial information you enter, the content of your conversations with the assistant, documents you choose to import (processed only, not stored), technical and diagnostic information, and in-app activity if you allow analytics. The sources are you and your phone; if you sign in with Google, also your Google account.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising based on your activity on other sites or apps. Gamora shows no ads.
  • Whatever state you live in, you can ask us what data we have, get a copy, correct it and delete it, and we will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. You can do so as described in sections 8 and 9; to protect your account we may confirm your identity using your account email.
  • Do Not Track: Gamora does not track your activity on other sites or apps, so it does not change how it works when it receives that signal.
  • Gamora is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data (see section 11).

11. MINORS

Gamora is intended for people 18 years of age or older. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it along with its data.

12. CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

We will publish any change in the app and on our website, with its update date and version number. If a change affects what data we process or why, we will ask you to accept the new version the next time you open the app; if you don't accept it, you can sign out and delete your account.
